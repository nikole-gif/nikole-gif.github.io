A fantasy romance series in progress, and the private writing tools built to keep its world consistent across sixteen books.

About this site

This page exists to support Oratherra, a personal, single-user application used by its author to organize manuscript and worldbuilding notes stored in her own Google Drive. The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.
