Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Oratherra ("the application") is a private, single-user writing tool operated by its sole author and user. This policy describes how it handles data.

Who operates the application

The application is operated by an individual for her own personal writing projects. It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and it has no users other than its operator.

What data the application accesses

With the operator's explicit consent, the application accesses files in the operator's own Google Drive account using the Google Drive API. This is limited to documents the operator creates for her own writing: manuscript drafts, worldbuilding notes, and reference material.

The application does not access any other Google service, and it does not access data belonging to anyone other than the operator.

How the data is used

Files are read, edited, and written back to the operator's Google Drive at her direction, in order to organize and revise her own manuscripts. That is the application's only function.

Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. No data is used for advertising. No analytics or tracking is performed.

Storage and retention

Working copies of the operator's documents, and the OAuth credentials used to access them, are stored on a private server controlled by the operator. Credentials are stored with file permissions restricted to the operator's own account. Both may be deleted by the operator at any time.

Revoking access

The operator may revoke the application's access to her Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Doing so immediately and permanently ends the application's ability to read or write any Drive data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to quillwieldinggirl@gmail.com.
